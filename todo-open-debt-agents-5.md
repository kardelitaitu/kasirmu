### How this phase was almost wrong — read this before touching one arm

The finding as it arrived from the cloud-sync retirement was *"CI cannot see these skips either; convert the arms to `#[ignore]`, the idiom `platform/sync` already uses."* **Both halves are false, and the second is the kind of change that quietly deletes coverage for a year.**
