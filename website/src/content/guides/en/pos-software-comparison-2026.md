Selecting the right Point of Sale (POS) application for a restaurant, cafe, convenience mart, or boutique retail store in Indonesia can feel overwhelming. Every software vendor claims their solution is the fastest, most feature-rich, and most affordable on the market. In the Indonesian retail technology landscape, three established market leaders dominate buyer searches: **Moka POS**, **Majoo**, and **Olsera**, alongside modern local-first challengers like **kasir.mu**.

However, daily reality at the checkout counter frequently diverges from glossy sales pitches. Some platforms charge steep monthly fees per outlet that balloon quickly, others freeze immediately when shop Wi-Fi experiences downtime, and some restrict basic transaction volume on budget pricing plans.

To guide your investment decision, this practical POS software comparison for 2026 evaluates Moka, Majoo, Olsera, and kasir.mu across four decisive operational criteria: pricing transparency, offline reliability, hardware flexibility, and daily cashier workflow speed.

## 1. Moka POS: The F&B Pioneer in the GoTo Ecosystem

Moka is an established pioneer of cloud-based POS software in Southeast Asia, now deeply embedded within the GoTo (Gojek-Tokopedia) digital ecosystem.

- **Key Strengths:** Exceptionally polished, intuitive user interface. Robust table layout management for dining establishments, comprehensive modifiers, and native integration with GoPay and GoFood marketing promotions.
- **Drawbacks & Trade-Offs:** The highest subscription pricing in its category, starting at approximately Rp249,000 to Rp299,000 (~$16–$19) per month **per outlet**. Multi-branch operations face substantial recurring software overhead. Furthermore, Moka relies heavily on cloud server connectivity; complex real-time sales reporting and cross-terminal synchronization degrade during network slowdowns.
- **Best Suited For:** Upscale cafes, full-service dining restaurants, and franchise chains with generous software budgets prioritizing GoFood delivery integration.

## 2. Majoo: The All-in-One Multi-Sector Business Suite

Majoo positions itself as a comprehensive entrepreneurial platform, bundling POS register functionality with light accounting, staff time tracking, and instant web storefronts.

- **Key Strengths:** Highly versatile feature catalog catering to varied commercial sectors (automotive repair, hair salons, laundry, and dry goods retail). Features automated employee attendance logging via camera verification and invoice generation.
- **Drawbacks & Trade-Offs:** Pricing starts around Rp199,000/month. Due to the sheer breadth of features, the user interface is dense, requiring longer onboarding and staff training times. Because core operations are fundamentally cloud-dependent, offline register functionality is restricted during extended internet blackouts.
- **Best Suited For:** Multi-service businesses and growing retail merchants seeking an all-in-one operational platform under a single subscription.

## 3. Olsera: The Omnichannel Marketplace Specialist

Olsera has built a strong industry reputation for bridging brick-and-mortar storefronts with Indonesian e-commerce marketplaces.

- **Key Strengths:** Seamless omnichannel inventory synchronization. For retailers operating a physical boutique while actively fulfilling orders on Shopee and Tokopedia, Olsera harmonizes stock levels in real time to prevent accidental overselling.
- **Drawbacks & Trade-Offs:** Standard packages begin at ~Rp199,000/month. Continuous multi-channel inventory synchronization demands unbroken high-speed internet bandwidth. When local network connections stutter during peak retail hours, automated marketplace updates can experience processing delays.
- **Best Suited For:** Fashion boutiques, beauty stores, and hobby shops balancing high-volume marketplace orders alongside foot traffic.

## 4. kasir.mu: The Resilient, Local-First Architecture

Unlike traditional platforms anchored to centralized remote cloud servers, kasir.mu is built from the ground up on a **Local-First & Offline-First** architecture.

- **Key Strengths:**
  1. **100% Uninterrupted Offline Operations:** Order processing, Bluetooth receipt printing, on-device inventory depletion, and Kitchen Display System (KDS) coordination operate without relying on external internet connectivity. Explore the technical design in our [offline POS app guide](/en/guides/offline-pos-app/).
  2. **Zero-Margin Erosion (Transparent Pricing):** An unlimited Free tier with zero product or transaction ceilings. Paid plans are fixed at an accessible Rp0 to Rp49,000/month, with zero hidden transaction cuts or revenue percentages.
  3. **Complete Hardware Freedom:** Operates smoothly on consumer Android smartphones, low-cost tablets, or Windows PCs without proprietary hardware locks.
- **Drawbacks & Trade-Offs:** Currently does not offer direct third-party e-commerce marketplace inventory sync like Olsera.
- **Best Suited For:** Independent warungs, neighborhood groceries, quick-serve coffee shops, and small businesses that demand instant checkout speed and zero vulnerability to internet blackouts.

## Summary Verdict: Which POS Fits Your Business?

1. **Choose Moka** if you run a premium dining restaurant with a dedicated tech budget and require deep GoFood ecosystem integration.
2. **Choose Majoo** if you operate a service-based business (like a salon or workshop) requiring staff payroll and invoicing tools in one package.
3. **Choose Olsera** if your primary operational bottleneck is reconciling physical boutique stock with active marketplace seller accounts.
4. **Choose kasir.mu** if your number-one priority is zero checkout downtime during internet outages, lightning-fast counter operations, and protecting store cash flow from aggressive monthly recurring fees.
